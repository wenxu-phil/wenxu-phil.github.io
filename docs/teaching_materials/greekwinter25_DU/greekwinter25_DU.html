Office Hours: Monday 4 - 6 PM (Sturm 263)

This course is designed to introduce to you major philosophical ideas originating in ancient Greece. Specifically, we will focus on six topics from ancient philosophy: (1) Happiness, (2) Virtue, (3) Democracy, (4) Love and Friendship, (5) Fate and Freedom, and (6) Ontology. Even nowadays problems and issues concerning these topics are still widely discussed and vigorously debated among people all over the world. In this course, my goal is to lead you to explore these big ideas in historical context and to have you reflect on your own beliefs and thoughts through an ancient lens. To achieve this goal, I will do my best to promote class discussion, intending to provoke questions and ideas in your mind. My hope is that you will come away with a deeper appreciation of what philosophy is, and why the Greeks are interesting.
